================
 Game Mechanics
================

Stats and Growth
----------------

"Lv1 Stats" shows the Robopon's stats when if is first sparked at Level 1.
Every level after that, its stats increase by the value in "LvUp Stats". So,
for example, a Robopon with an ATK of 20 at Level 1, and a growth of 2 would
have an ATK of 38 at level 10.

HP and EP are capped at 999, and all other stats are capped at 255. However,
you can push stats over 255 with equipment, meaning equipment is still useful
for Robopon at the stat cap.

When a Robopon goes over its "Max level", its stats other than HP and EP stop
growing even though its level still increases as normal.

Enhancing
---------

Enhancing a Robopon halves its level and reduces its stats to 2/3 of their
value before Enhancing (rounding down for both), after which it grows like a
normal Robopon of its type.

Robopon keep all skills they know when they Enhance. Thus, even though Diane
doesn't learn Nova{Hate}, it will know it if it was Enhanced from Sherry.

Special Traits & Resistances
----------------------------

There are a few traits of Robopon that aren't shown in the game's stat screen.
For example, some Robopon use less EP for any skills they use, and others
have a chance of counterattacking when someone does a normal attack on them.

In addition, all Robopon have hidden vulnerability values to different
elements. A Robopon with a 125% vulnerability to an element will take 125%
damage from it, and one with a 0% vulnerability is immune to it.

Status is somewhat different. A 50% vulnerability means that a status spell
with a 50% chance of working will work 25% on it, and a 200% vulnerability to
status means that skill will work 100% of the time.

Obtaining Robopon
-----------------

Besides the obvious means like sparking, or enhancing Robopon from previous
versions, there are other ways to acquire Robopon.

Fossilized Robopon can be found around the world. They can be revived with
the spirit Takako from Red Mushrooms. To get this spirit, go into the small
forest in the path that branches off from the path to the circus, and talk to
the man there with a Red Mushroom in your inventory.

Some Robopon can be acquired by talking to a dog behind Marvel Labs, and
entering a password. This lab can be found near Neon City after you become
Master and get the XStone1.

Finally, all Robopon can be Link Sparked if you have a friend with the game,
or two copies of it except Wobble (for some reason), BigZap, Icarus, and one
of either Bulbot or Gear (depending on your version).

Hit Chance
----------

A Robopon's chance of hitting another depends on its speed. For every point
of speed the defender has over the attacker, there's an additional 1/175
chance that the attacker will miss.

In addition, equipment have a hidden stat that modifies a Robopon's hit and
evasion rate. When attacking, Robopon add the equipment's hidden accuracy
rating to their speed, and when defending, Robopon add the equipment's hidden
evasion rating to their speed.

Software skills that use FOR and WIL for damage instead of ATK and DEF have
a hit rate that changes on the skill, regardless of the attacker or defender's
stats.

Misc. Formulas
--------------

Normal attack damage: base skill damage +
                      (attacker's ATK + attacker's level) -
                      (2/3 * defender's DEF + 1/3 * defender's level)
Magic attack damage : base skill damage +
                      (attacker's FOR - defender's WIL) / 5

===============
 Robopon Stats
===============

How to read
-----------
[ Robopon description ]

Type      : The Robopon's type and blood type
Weigt/Size: The Robopon's weight and size class
Max Level : After this level, the Robopon's stats stop increasing
EXP Curve : How fast the Robopon levels up. See Experience Curves chart
Lv1  Stats: The Robopon's stats at level 1
LvUp Stats: The increase in a Robopon's stats every time it levels up
Max  Stats: Maximum possible stats for a Robopon.
Software  : What software this Robopon learns, and at what levels
Special   : Special traits this Robopon has
Vulnerblty: How vulnerable this Robopon is to damage types/status effects
Enhances  : What this Robopon enhances to

Obtaining : How to get this Robopon
Link Spark: How to link spark this Robopon
Drops     : The items this Robopon may drop on being defeated.
Steal Drop: The items you can obtain by using Steal on this Robopon.
